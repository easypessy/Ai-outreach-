🎯 AI Outreach Finder

Find local businesses that actually need a specific AI service → see their real contact details → find who makes the decision → copy a personalised pitch. Built for selling the 10 AI automations.

💸 100% free on your end — no API keys, no cards, no credits. Data: OpenStreetMap (free public API).
⚡ How it works (and why it's completely free)

1. Discovery — the app queries OpenStreetMap's free public API for real, mapped businesses of the type you pick, in the city and radius you set. It pulls their name, address, phone, email, website and social profiles — all real data you can verify.

  • Does it guess or verify they need it? Both — and it tells you which. The app live-checks each lead's website (✅): it reads their homepage and flags whether they already run a chat widget (Tidio, Intercom, Drift…), whether they have a booking system (Calendly, Booksy…), and how many help/support pages they run. That's real evidence, not a guess. On top of that it adds signals (⚡) from map data (has a phone, active socials, known buyer type) and points you at 10-second manual checks (🔎) like their Google review responses. The fit score blends ✅ + ⚡; 🔎 items are the proof that turns "probably" into "yes, they need it".
  • Decision-maker — the app tells you the most likely role (e.g. Office Manager for a dental office), and if their website has a Team/About page it will often pull a real person's name + title from it. One-click LinkedIn / Google / Facebook searches are pre-filled with their business name. You open those in your own free account.
  • Pitches — every lead gets a ready email + DM personalised with their real name, city, phone/website. Only [REVIEW COUNT] and [Your name] are left for you to fill.

2. It stays free — Overpass API and Nominatim are free and keyless. No paid LLM: pitches come from a built-in template engine. LinkedIn/Google searches run in your own free browser account.

3. Tips — start with radius 3–5 km in a dense downtown; use the Shortlist ★ button and then Export CSV to track who you've contacted. For B2B services (7, 8, 10), use the extra LinkedIn company search on each card to also target bigger firms.
